Guided Exercise: Locate Files on the System

Q1: Prepare the lab environment and log in to servera as student.
A: lab start fs-locate
   ssh student@servera

Q2: Update the locate database and search for logrotate.conf and networkmanager.conf (case-insensitive).
A: sudo updatedb
   locate logrotate.conf
   locate -i networkmanager.conf

Q3: Find files in /var/lib owned by chrony, and files in /var owned by root and group mail.
A: sudo find /var/lib -user chrony
   sudo find /var -user root -group mail

Q4: Find files in /usr/bin larger than 50 KB, files in /home/student modified in the last 120 mins, and block devices in /dev.
A: find /usr/bin -size +50k
   find /home/student -mmin -120
   find /dev -type b

Q5: Exit servera and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: exit
   lab finish fs-locate
