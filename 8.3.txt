Guided Exercise: Monitor Process Activity

Q1: Prepare the lab environment and open two terminals logged in to servera as student.
A: lab start processes-monitor
   ssh student@servera

Q2: Create /home/student/bin/monitor CPU-load script and make it executable.
A: mkdir /home/student/bin
   vim /home/student/bin/monitor
   chmod a+x /home/student/bin/monitor

Q3: Launch top in right terminal and check CPU count in left terminal.
A: top   (in right terminal; toggle headers with l, t, m)
   lscpu

Q4: Start six instances of monitor in the background to push 1-minute load average above 1.
A: monitor &   (repeat 6 times)

Q5: Terminate all monitor processes interactively inside top.
A: In top: press k -> enter PID -> press Enter -> confirm signal 15 (SIGTERM)

Q6: Quit top (q), exit servera, and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: q
   exit
   lab finish processes-monitor
