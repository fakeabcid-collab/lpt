Guided Exercise: Make Links Between Files

Q1: Prepare the lab environment on workstation.
A: lab start files-make

Q2: Log in to servera as student.
A: ssh student@servera

Q3: Check inode/link count of files/target.file, create hard link links/file.hardlink, and verify.
A: ls -li files/target.file
   ln files/target.file links/file.hardlink
   ls -li files/target.file links/file.hardlink

Q4: Create symbolic link /home/student/tempdir pointing to /tmp and verify.
A: ln -s /tmp /home/student/tempdir
   ls -li /home/student/tempdir

Q5: Exit servera and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: exit
   lab finish files-make
