Guided Exercise: Control Jobs

Q1: Prepare the lab environment and open two terminals logged in to servera as student.
A: lab start processes-control
   ssh student@servera

Q2: Create /home/student/bin/control script and make it executable.
A: mkdir /home/student/bin
   vim /home/student/bin/control   (loop appending "$@" to ~/control_outfile every 1s)
   chmod +x /home/student/bin/control

Q3: Run 'control technical' in foreground, monitor in second terminal with tail -f, then suspend with Ctrl+Z.
A: control technical
   tail -f ~/control_outfile   (in right terminal)
   Ctrl+Z

Q4: View jobs and resume the stopped job in the background.
A: jobs
   bg

Q5: Start two more instances in the background using '&'.
A: control documents &
   control database &
   jobs

Q6: Bring job %1 to foreground and suspend it (Ctrl+Z); bring job %2 to foreground and terminate it (Ctrl+C).
A: fg %1   (then Ctrl+Z)
   fg %2   (then Ctrl+C)

Q7: View process states (T = stopped, S = sleeping), terminate remaining job %3, and clean up.
A: ps jT
   fg %3   (then Ctrl+C)
   rm ~/control_outfile

Q8: Exit servera and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: exit
   lab finish processes-control
