Guided Exercise: Manage Local User Accounts

Q1: Prepare the lab environment, log in to servera, and switch to root.
A: lab start users-user
   ssh student@servera
   sudo -i

Q2: Create users operator1, operator2, and operator3, and set their passwords to redhat.
A: useradd operator1 && passwd operator1
   useradd operator2 && passwd operator2
   useradd operator3 && passwd operator3

Q3: Add comments "Operator One" and "Operator Two" to operator1 and operator2, and verify.
A: usermod -c "Operator One" operator1
   usermod -c "Operator Two" operator2
   tail /etc/passwd

Q4: Delete operator3 along with its home directory and verify removal.
A: userdel -r operator3
   tail /etc/passwd
   ls -l /home

Q5: Exit back to workstation and finish the lab exercise.
A: exit
   exit
   lab finish users-user
