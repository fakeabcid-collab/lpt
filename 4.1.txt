Guided Exercise: Read Manual Pages

Q1: Prepare the lab environment on workstation.

Q2: View the gedit man page and open /home/student/manual with cursor at the end of the file.

   gedit + manual

Q3: Read section 1 of the su man page.
A: man 1 su

Q4: Open the manual page for the man command itself.
A: man man
