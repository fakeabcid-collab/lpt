Guided Exercise: Identify Automatically Started System Processes

Q1: Prepare the lab environment and log in to servera as student.
A: lab start services-identify
   ssh student@servera

Q2: List all installed active service units and all socket units (active and inactive).
A: systemctl list-units --type=service
   systemctl list-units --type=socket --all

Q3: Check status of chronyd and verify its daemon PID with ps.
A: systemctl status chronyd
   ps -p <PID>

Q4: Check if sshd is enabled and active, then view its detailed status.
A: systemctl is-enabled sshd
   systemctl is-active sshd
   systemctl status sshd

Q5: List enabled/disabled states for all service unit files.
A: systemctl list-unit-files --type=service

Q6: Exit servera and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: exit
   lab finish services-identify
