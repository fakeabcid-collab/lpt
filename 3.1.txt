Guided Exercise: Manage Files with Command-line Tools

Q1: Prepare the lab environment on workstation.
A: lab start files-manage

Q2: Log in to servera as student and create Music, Pictures, and Videos directories.
A: ssh student@servera
   mkdir Music Pictures Videos

Q3: Create 6 empty files each for songX.mp3, snapX.jpg, and filmX.avi.
A: touch song1.mp3 song2.mp3 song3.mp3 song4.mp3 song5.mp3 song6.mp3
   touch snap1.jpg snap2.jpg snap3.jpg snap4.jpg snap5.jpg snap6.jpg
   touch film1.avi film2.avi film3.avi film4.avi film5.avi film6.avi

Q4: Move .mp3 files to Music, .jpg files to Pictures, and .avi files to Videos.
A: mv song1.mp3 song2.mp3 song3.mp3 song4.mp3 song5.mp3 song6.mp3 Music
   mv snap1.jpg snap2.jpg snap3.jpg snap4.jpg snap5.jpg snap6.jpg Pictures
   mv film1.avi film2.avi film3.avi film4.avi film5.avi film6.avi Videos

Q5: Create friends, family, and work subdirectories in a single command.
A: mkdir friends family work

Q6: Copy files containing numbers 1 and 2 to friends, and 3 and 4 to family.
A: cd friends
   cp ~/Music/song1.mp3 ~/Music/song2.mp3 ~/Pictures/snap1.jpg ~/Pictures/snap2.jpg ~/Videos/film1.avi ~/Videos/film2.avi .
   cd ../family
   cp ~/Music/song3.mp3 ~/Music/song4.mp3 ~/Pictures/snap3.jpg ~/Pictures/snap4.jpg ~/Videos/film3.avi ~/Videos/film4.avi .

Q7: Copy family and friends directories with contents into work.
A: cd ../work
   cp -r ~/family ~/friends .

Q8: Recursively delete family, friends, and work directories, then exit servera.
A: cd ..
   rm -r family friends work
   exit

Q9: Finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: lab finish files-manage
