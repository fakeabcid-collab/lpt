Guided Exercise: Control System Services

Q1: Prepare the lab environment, log in to servera, and switch to root.
A: lab start services-control
   ssh student@servera
   sudo -i

Q2: Restart sshd (changes PID) and then reload sshd (keeps same PID).
A: systemctl restart sshd
   systemctl reload sshd
   systemctl status sshd

Q3: Stop chronyd, verify it is still enabled, and reboot servera to confirm it starts on boot.
A: systemctl stop chronyd
   systemctl is-enabled chronyd
   systemctl reboot

Q4: Log back in as root, disable chronyd, and reboot to confirm it stays inactive after boot.
A: ssh student@servera && sudo -i
   systemctl disable chronyd
   systemctl reboot

Q5: Verify chronyd is inactive, exit servera, and finish the lab exercise.
A: ssh student@servera
   systemctl status chronyd
   exit
   lab finish services-control
