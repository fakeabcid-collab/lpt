Lab: Configure and Secure SSH

Q1: Prepare the lab environment, log in to servera, and switch to production1 (password: redhat).
A: lab start ssh-review
   ssh student@servera
   su - production1

Q2: Generate passphrase-less SSH keys for production1 and copy the public key to production1@serverb.
A: ssh-keygen
   ssh-copy-id production1@serverb

Q3: Log in to serverb as production1, switch to root (password: redhat), disable root login & password auth, and reload sshd.
A: ssh production1@serverb
   su -
   vim /etc/ssh/sshd_config   (set: PermitRootLogin no and PasswordAuthentication no)
   systemctl reload sshd

Q4: Exit all shells back to workstation, then grade and finish the lab exercise.
A: exit
   exit
   exit
   exit
   lab grade ssh-review
   lab finish ssh-review
