Guided Exercise: Access the Command Line with the Desktop

Q1: Prepare the lab environment on workstation.
A: lab start cli-desktop

Q2: Log in to workstation as student.
A: Click 'student' at GNOME login screen -> Enter password: student -> Press Enter

Q3: Open Terminal and change student password to 55TurnK3y.
A: passwd
   (Current password: student | New password: 55TurnK3y | Retype: 55TurnK3y)

Q4: Log out and log back in as student with the new password.
A: System menu (top-right) -> Power Off/Log Out -> Log Out -> Log in with 55TurnK3y

Q5: Lock and unlock the screen.
A: Lock: System menu -> Lock button
   Unlock: Press Enter -> Enter password: 55TurnK3y -> Press Enter

Q6: Initiate shutdown from GUI and cancel it.
A: System menu -> Power Off/Log Out -> Power Off -> Click Cancel

Q7: Complete and clean up the lab exercise.
A: lab finish cli-desktop
