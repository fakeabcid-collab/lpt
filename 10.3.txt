Guided Exercise: Customize OpenSSH Service Configuration

Q1: Prepare the lab environment, log in to serverb, and switch to operator2 (password: redhat).
A: lab start ssh-customize
   ssh student@serverb
   su - operator2

Q2: Generate passphrase-less SSH keys for operator2 and copy the public key to operator2@servera.
A: ssh-keygen
   ssh-copy-id operator2@servera

Q3: Log in to servera as operator2, switch to root (password: redhat), disable root SSH login, and reload sshd.
A: ssh operator2@servera
   su -
   vim /etc/ssh/sshd_config   (set: PermitRootLogin no)
   systemctl reload sshd

Q4: Disable SSH password authentication on servera, reload sshd, and verify key-only login works.
A: vim /etc/ssh/sshd_config   (set: PasswordAuthentication no)
   systemctl reload sshd

Q5: Exit all shells back to workstation and finish the lab exercise.
A: exit
   exit
   exit
   lab finish ssh-customize
