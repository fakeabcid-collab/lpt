Lab: Manage Users and Groups, Permissions, and Processes (Comprehensive Review 2)

Q1: Prepare the lab environment and log in to serverb as student.
A: lab start rhcsa-rh124-review2
   ssh student@serverb

Q2: Identify and terminate the highest CPU-consuming process (dd) using top.
A: top   (press k -> enter PID of dd -> confirm signal 15 -> press q)

Q3: Switch to root, create group 'database' (GID 50000) and user 'dbadmin1' with supplementary group database.
A: sudo -i
   groupadd -g 50000 database
   useradd -G database dbadmin1
   passwd dbadmin1   (set to redhat)

Q4: Configure dbadmin1 password policy (force change on login, min 10 days, max 30 days), sudo access, and umask 007.
A: chage -d 0 -m 10 -M 30 dbadmin1
   echo "dbadmin1 ALL=(ALL) ALL" > /etc/sudoers.d/dbadmin1
   su - dbadmin1
   echo "umask 007" >> ~/.bashrc && source ~/.bashrc

Q5: Create /home/dbadmin1/review2 owned by :database with rwxrwsr-t (3775) and set /home/dbadmin1 to o+x (701).
A: mkdir /home/dbadmin1/review2
   chown :database /home/dbadmin1/review2
   chmod 3775 /home/dbadmin1/review2
   chmod o+x /home/dbadmin1

Q6: Exit all shells back to workstation, then grade and finish the lab exercise.
A: exit
   exit
   exit
   lab grade rhcsa-rh124-review2
   lab finish rhcsa-rh124-review2
