Lab: Control Services and Daemons

Q1: Prepare the lab environment, log in to serverb, and switch to root.
A: lab start services-review
   ssh student@serverb
   sudo -i

Q2: Start the psacct service and enable it to start at boot.
A: systemctl start psacct
   systemctl enable psacct

Q3: Stop the rsyslog service and disable it from starting at boot.
A: systemctl stop rsyslog
   systemctl disable rsyslog

Q4: Reboot serverb, then grade and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: systemctl reboot
   lab grade services-review
   lab finish services-review
