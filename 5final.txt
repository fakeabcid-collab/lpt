Lab: Create, View, and Edit Text Files

Q1: Prepare the lab environment on workstation.
A: lab start edit-review

Q2: Assign editing_final_lab.txt to lab_file variable and redirect detailed directory listing into it.
A: lab_file=editing_final_lab.txt
   ls -al > $lab_file

Q3: Edit $lab_file in Vim using visual modes (Shift+V for lines, v for characters, Ctrl+V for blocks).
A: vim $lab_file
   (Shift+V to remove first 3 lines & Desktop/Public lines; v / Ctrl+V to trim columns & timestamps; :wq to save)

Q4: Back up editing_final_lab.txt with an epoch timestamp suffix.
A: cp $lab_file editing_final_lab_$(date +%s).txt

Q5: Append 12 dashes to $lab_file.
A: echo "------------" >> $lab_file

Q6: List Documents/ contents, append to $lab_file while displaying on screen, and verify.
A: ls Documents/ | tee -a $lab_file
   cat $lab_file

Q7: Grade and finish the lab exercise on workstation.
A: lab grade edit-review
   lab finish edit-review
